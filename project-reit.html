The question

Federal Realty Investment Trust (FRT) owns open-air shopping centers across the U.S. I wanted to answer a real investment question: where should it expand next?

What I built

  • Pulled all 107 portfolio properties from FRT's FY2025 10-K into a proprietary dataset.
  • Joined Census demographic and economic data to ~1,400 ZIP-code-level submarkets and scored each one on expansion fit.
  • Framed site selection as a data-driven investment question instead of a gut call.

Why it's built this way

The model is a demographic and economic screen. It deliberately leaves out property-level operating metrics like rent and occupancy, which would leak the answer into the inputs. I chose an interpretable rationale over a black-box score.
